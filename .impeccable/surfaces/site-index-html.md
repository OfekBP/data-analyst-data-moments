# Surface brief: site/index.html (the submission page)

Scope and mode: Read, framed by the brand (a document the reviewer must understand fast). One page, Hebrew RTL, static, deployed on Vercel.

Audience and job: Data Moments hiring team verifying a take-home. They scan answers, open evidence, check the repo. Constraint: equivalent of two pages; every brief requirement present: 5 questions, data quality, assumptions, AI use and verification, link to calculations.

Brief-pinned world: Data Moments' identity (user's explicit request): `#131313` bar, deep purple `#4A3F78`, cream `#FCFDF4`, teal-lavender gradient, Rubik, pill buttons. No direction roll: a pinned brief beats it.

Memorable moment: each question is a row with its one-line answer always visible; opening it sweeps open the evidence with a small interactive chart (store metric switcher, mean-median gap funnel, Q1-vs-Q4 bars, Haifa-vs-rest dumbbell).

Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A submission that behaves like an analyst's deliverable: answer first, evidence on demand. Refuses the generic landing page (hero metrics, icon cards, testimonial strip) and the long scrolling report.

OWN-WORLD: Data Moments' own system. A near-black masthead, a deep-blue/violet field behind a cream hero card with purple Rubik headline and a purple pill button; cream ground, purple ink, teal-lavender gradient as surfaces and bars (never on text), 20px radii, thin purple hairlines. Recognisable with all content removed.

STORY: The reviewer understands in ten seconds that the candidate found one concrete, quantified thing (Haifa sells the most and earns the least per shekel, a mix problem worth about 54 thousand ILS), believes it because each number can be opened and traced to code, and goes to GitHub or opens a question.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead with logo (start side) and anchors plus GitHub pill. Below it the dark violet field with a centred cream card: answer-first headline (Haifa sells most, earns least per shekel), one sentence of context, two pills (to the answers, to GitHub). The first answer row peeks under the card on desktop.

FORM: Accordion document: five question rows plus data quality, AI and verification, assumptions; one-line answer in every collapsed row; expand-all control; deep links; print expands everything. Position 1 of the brief-pinned form. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
